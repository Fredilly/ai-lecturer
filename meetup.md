<div class="card">
  <h2>建立人工智能社区 / Building an AI Community in Western China</h2>
  <p>In 2015, I founded what became <strong>one of Chengdu's earliest dedicated AI and Machine Learning meetup groups</strong>. At a time when AI communities were concentrated in Beijing, Shanghai, and Shenzhen, building a technical AI community in Chengdu — a major city in western China — required vision, persistence, and genuine enthusiasm for the subject.</p>
  <p>The meetup ran successfully for three years (2015–2018), growing into a sustained community that brought together students, professionals, researchers, and tech enthusiasts interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science.</p>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>发生了什么 / What the Meetup Did</h2>

  <h3>会议组织 / Meeting Organization</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Curated technical topics</strong> spanning the AI/ML landscape — from foundational machine learning concepts to deep learning, NLP, computer vision, data science, and real-world AI applications</li>
    <li><strong>Regularly scheduled meetings</strong> — consistent, predictable gatherings that built a reliable community rhythm and allowed members to plan their participation</li>
    <li><strong>Hands-on and theoretical content</strong> — balancing conceptual understanding with practical demonstrations, code walkthroughs, and discussion of real implementations</li>
    <li><strong>Progressive difficulty</strong> — sessions ranged from accessible introductions for newcomers to advanced technical deep-dives for experienced practitioners</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>演讲者策招 / Speaker Curation</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>邀请Google工程师</strong>参讲 — bringing international tech industry perspectives to the Chengdu AI community</li>
    <li><strong>邀请中国人工智能研究人员</strong>参讲 — connecting the community with domestic AI research expertise from Chinese universities and research institutions</li>
    <li><strong>构建国际与本土 expertise 的桥梁</strong> — creating a space where international and Chinese AI knowledge and experience could meet and cross-pollinate</li>
    <li><strong>多元化的演讲者阵容</strong> — from industry practitioners to academic researchers to independent enthusiasts, giving the community broad exposure to different AI perspectives</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>社区管理 / Community Management</h3>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>从零开始建设社区</strong> — building membership, establishing norms, creating a welcoming environment for people at all levels of AI/ML experience</li>
    <li><strong>跨文化社区</strong> — bringing together Chinese and international community members, facilitating communication across language and cultural boundaries</li>
    <li><strong>持续运营三年</strong> — maintaining momentum, engagement, and quality over a multi-year period, which requires genuine commitment and organizational skill</li>
    <li><strong>知识分享文化</strong> — fostering an environment where members shared knowledge, asked questions, collaborated, and learned from each other</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="card">
  <h2>为什么这对JISU的职位很重要 / Why This Matters for the JISU Position</h2>

  <p>Jilin International Studies University describes itself as a bridge between Chinese and Western cultures, aspiring to help students "cross and assimilate Chinese and Western cultures, and thus become an integral part of the world at large." The university prides itself on international exchange programs and fostering international relations.</p>

  <p>My experience building and leading the Chengdu AI Meetup directly demonstrates the kind of <strong>bridge-building, cross-cultural community leadership, and commitment to knowledge sharing</strong> that aligns with JISU's institutional mission:</p>

  <ul>
    <li><strong>证明了搭建中西文化与技术交流桥梁的能力</strong> — The meetup brought together Chinese and international expertise in AI, creating a space for cross-cultural learning and exchange in a field that itself transcends national boundaries</li>
    <li><strong>展示了知识分享的承诺和能力</strong> — Building a community around AI education and knowledge sharing demonstrates a genuine commitment to making technical knowledge accessible — the same spirit that drives university teaching</li>
    <li><strong>证明了社区组织和管理能力</strong> — Running a successful meetup for three years requires organizational skill, communication ability, and the capacity to build and sustain engagement — all transferable to university teaching, student engagement, and academic community building</li>
    <li><strong>体现了对人工智能教育的 long-term 承诺</strong> — I was interested in AI education and community building well before it was mainstream — this genuine, long-standing enthusiasm for AI comes through in teaching</li>
    <li><strong>与JISU的国际化使命相呼应</strong> — A university that values international exchange and bridge-building between cultures would value a faculty member who has actually done that in the AI space</li>
  </ul>
</div>
